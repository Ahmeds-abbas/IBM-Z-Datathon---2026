05 / How to interpret the results

Privacy matters. So does context.

Protected identifiers

The example uses HMAC-derived account codes rather than real account numbers. Pseudonymisation is not anonymity, and the three bank groups are simulated.

Historical, not live

V3 uses recorded historical decision-time snapshots. Its XGBoost model was trained in 2026 and was not deployed in 2022. The V2 ring network is a separate retrospective example. No live bank feed is connected.

Human-led decisions

A risk score is not proof of laundering. Amounts span multiple currencies, and no customer funds are frozen or recovered by this interface.
